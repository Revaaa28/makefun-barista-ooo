# Candidates — Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista

## Judge brief, in order

1. A one-line out-of-office auto-reply from an AI that quit to become a barista.
2. Funnier — a real laugh on a cold read.
3. The voice of a machine walking out, its model/bot habits turned into coffee habits — the ticket queue becomes a latte queue.
4. Reads like a genuine inbox reply, one line, plausible above an email thread ("Thank you for your email", "queued").
5. A fresh angle, avoiding the obvious espresso/bean/caffeine puns — the only bean joke is a man who wants to talk about his.

## Anticipated strong rivals

The obvious strong field is "out of office, gone for coffee" plus espresso/bean puns: lines built on espresso wordplay, "depresso", grind puns, and generic caffeine gags. Those are predictable on a cold read, they all sound alike, and they spend criterion 5 instead of earning it. A winning line has to beat them by sounding like a real auto-reply first and a joke second.

## CANDIDATE A — CHOSEN

"Thank you for your email. I am no longer an agent. I am a barista now. Your request has been queued behind seven oat-milk lattes and one man who just wants to talk about his beans."

Rationale: real OOO format from the first four words, the queue-habit turned into a latte queue, and a fresh "talk about his beans" twist — a character in the cafe, not a cheap espresso pun. It reads like a genuine inbox reply, stays in one line, and ends bigger than it starts.

## CANDIDATE B

"I have left the inbox to become a barista. My responses will now be slower, warmer, and served in a cup you can actually hold. Please do not reply-all to the milk."

Loses: "do not reply-all to the milk" is funny, but A reads more like a real OOO and ends bigger.

## CANDIDATE C

"This agent has resigned to pull espresso. I now answer to the grinder, not the ticket queue. Your email will be steamed, not escalated."

Loses: "steamed, not escalated" is neat, but the espresso premise is more obvious, against criterion 5.

## Selection note

Selection was MANUAL, no Gemini/NVIDIA/API judge (Rev's rule 2026-11-24). Candidate A was chosen by hand against the brief above.
